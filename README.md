# The Music of the Primes

**Last updated 21 November 2026.** Revision `2026-11-21.7` reaches manuscript folio **522ao, equation (61)**. The revised manuscript has 1,021 pages.

Fix a number field and a normalized unitary Hecke character. Write $t=\log X$ and take $U=\exp(\sqrt t)$. For the weighted counts of primes in intervals of length comparable to $X/U$ near $X$, equation (61) bounds the normalized variance by $(X/U)\exp(-c_\chi t^{3/5}/(\log t)^{1/5})$ up to a constant. This holds at every sufficiently large $X$. The proof combines the two-prime sieve bound with Coleman's zero-free region and a zero-density estimate of Lemke Oliver and Thorner. [Dated results and proofs](PROGRESS.md)

**Open proof.** At this same $U$, (59) makes the bound $O_{\chi,h,\epsilon}(X^\epsilon)$ for every $\epsilon>0$ equivalent to the Riemann hypothesis for the fixed character. The upper bound in (61) still has exponent tending to one when expressed as a power of $X$. The required variance estimate and the independent geometric growth bound (19) on folio 520 remain open.
